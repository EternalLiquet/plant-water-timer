A little help

Start with a photo

Choose a clear leaf or whole-plant photo. Pick a likely match, or enter the name yourself. AI can be wrong, especially with similar plants.

When to water

The date is a reminder to check the soil. Water only when your plant needs it, then tap Watered today. Use Undo if you tap by mistake.

Keep it on your phone

Use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app” option. Your garden's server must be running and reachable to save changes. Nothing is saved while offline.

Your photos stay private

Photos and plant history are stored on this garden's server. Location metadata is removed. No photos are sent to an outside AI service. Ask the person running your garden about backups and removing your data.

One private garden per installation. Don't share its password with people you don't want to give access.
