How it works

Check, then water

Each plant gets a day to check on it. When that day comes, push a finger about an inch into the soil. If it's dry, water the plant and tap Watered today. If it's still damp, leave it and look again tomorrow.

Adding a plant

A photo is optional. If you add one, you'll see a few guesses at what kind of plant it is. Guesses can be wrong, so pick one only if it looks right, or type your own.

Fixing mistakes

Tapped Watered today by accident? Tap Undo. Forgot to record a watering? Add it under History. To rename a plant, change how often to check it, or remove it, tap Edit.

Put it on your home screen

In your phone's browser menu, choose Add to Home Screen. You need to be online to save changes.

Your privacy

Your plants and photos stay on your own garden's computer. Location details are removed from photos, and photos are never sent to an outside service. Anyone with the garden password can see and change your plants.
